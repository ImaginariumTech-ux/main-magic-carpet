## HOW WE WORK

1.  Reach out: You send us a message using the form in our "Work with
    us" page (Link to page)

2.  Discovery call: We call you to understand your idea, audience, and
    final goal

3.  Proposal: We send you a proposal with a quote and a timeline within
    48 hours

4.  Contract: Once we are agreed, we draw up a contract

5.  Onboard: We introduce you to the team that will work on your project
    and how communication will flow

6.  Production: Concept, script, storyboard, design, animation. You
    review at every stage

7.  Delivery: We deliver your completed project in the format(s) you
    require

8.  Review: If you love our work and production, you are welcome to give
    us a review and refer us
